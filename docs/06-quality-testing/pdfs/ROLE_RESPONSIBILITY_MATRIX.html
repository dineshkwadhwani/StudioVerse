Summary by Section

Sections covered: 21 functional areas (16 existing + 5 new since the prior matrix: Search, Messaging, Notifications, Buy Credits, Revenue Routing).

Major capability adds since last revision:

  • E16 Search & User Discovery — universal search across catalog + users with association protection.
  • E16 Messaging — intro messages with templates, locked/unlocked states, paid unlock.
  • E14 Lead Config — per-tenant per-type lead unlock fees driving search & messaging.
  • E14 Revenue Routing — creator earnings, company association routing, self-assignment treasury rule.
  • E13 Buy Credits — placeholder package purchase flow with order lifecycle.
  • Notification system — admin-configurable categories & reminders.
  • Bot Hero monetization — full lifecycle (request → approval → activation → expiry) with widget override.

Known gaps (not in MVP, flagged for roadmap):

  • Real PSP integration (Razorpay) for Buy Credits.
  • Coach/Company-side Program/Event creation UI.
  • AI assessment-question generation UI.
  • Listing fee charge on public content publish.
  • Marketplace commission split (creator + platform).
  • Route-level role guards (today only menu-level hiding).
  • SEO generateMetadata() on landing.
  • Rate limiting on bot endpoints.
